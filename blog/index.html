DisCo Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026

About

The DisCo workshop aims to bring together the community working on continuous flows and diffusion for discrete generation. In this blogpost track, we encourage transparent discussions and opinions in the field and make this emerging area more accessible to a wider audience.

Call for Blogposts

We invite blogpost submissions on topics related to continuous flows and diffusion for discrete data. Blogposts are a great format for:

  • Accessible introductions or tutorials on key methods in the field.
  • Critical analyses, comparisons, or opinions on recent papers.
  • Empirical insights, negative results, or practical tips that may not warrant a full paper.
  • Distillations of technical ideas aimed at a broader ML audience.
  • Reflections on open problems and future directions.

Submission format: Max 2,000 words. Posts should be self-contained and accessible to a general ML audience.

Deadline: August 29, 2026 (AoE), same as the paper track.

Notification: September 26, 2026.

Formatting Guidelines

Accepted blogposts will be published on this page. Submissions should follow these guidelines:

  • Posts should be written in clear, accessible English.
  • Use figures, diagrams, or equations where they help convey the main ideas.
  • Include proper citations and references to related work.
  • Posts may include interactive elements (e.g., embedded visualizations) where appropriate.
  • All submissions will be reviewed for clarity, relevance, and quality of exposition.
